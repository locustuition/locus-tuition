About

Teaching maths with clarity & care

Hi, I'm Miss Kim - a maths teacher who cares as much about the why behind a concept as the how.

I've taught maths for more than ten years, including as Head of Maths - so I know the syllabus from Year 3 through to Extension 2, and I know where students tend to come unstuck long before they get there.

Every student here gets patient, structured support built around what they actually need - whether they're laying foundations in Year 3 or sitting down to their HSC papers. Lessons are small by design, so nobody spends an hour quietly stuck.

I write all of my own booklets. Each one is mapped to the NSW syllabus, built up in small steps, and written in plain language - with the common mistakes named on the page, because knowing the trap is half of avoiding it.
